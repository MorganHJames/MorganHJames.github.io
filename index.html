About Me

Since 2016, I've been driven by one goal: to make technology feel immersive, intuitive, and human. As a full-stack Unity developer, I've spent the last nine years creating VR simulations, games, and interactive applications that teach, train, and inspire.

My skill set spans the entire development pipeline — from UX and visual design, to front-end C# and Unity, to robust backend systems integration (Azure, REST APIs, PostgreSQL). I thrive on bringing every layer of a project together to build polished, seamless, effective experiences.

In 2024 I was honoured as the Most Recognised Mentor at the MIT Reality Hackathon, where I helped teams bring complex XR visions to life. I'm actively seeking full-time Senior Unity / XR opportunities where I can contribute deep technical expertise to impactful immersive products.

Mogi's Inferno

Epic MegaJam 2025 · Unreal Engine 5

Created for the Epic MegaJam 2025 with the theme "Here We Go Again," Mogi's Inferno is an intense escape challenge where players must learn and optimise their route through multiple attempts. Starting at the entrance of an apartment building, players have just 3 minutes to reach the evacuation helicopter waiting on the roof.

Through each "failed" attempt, players discover critical shortcuts, item locations, and hazards to avoid, gradually building the knowledge needed to complete a successful speedrun.
